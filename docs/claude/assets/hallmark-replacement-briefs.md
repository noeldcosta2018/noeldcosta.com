## Shared art direction

Follows the agentic AI security hero (`/images/agentic-ai-security/hero.webp`).

- Editorial still-life photograph, soft daylight, warm off-white backdrop.
- Natural materials: concrete, wood, paper, steel, card.
- One orange element as the only accent colour.
- Calm composition with empty space, shallow depth of field, 16:9.
- No people or hands, no screens or devices, no charts, no readable text,
  no logos, no robots, no glowing effects.
- The image is a metaphor for the work, never a claim about the client.
