## Sources used

Cost ranges by size (research requested by Noel):
1. LeverX, "SAP S/4HANA implementation cost guide UK" (published 27 Jul 2026, updated 11 Oct 2026): https://leverx.com/en-gb/blog/sap-s4hana-implementation-cost-guide-uk. Executive summary bands: £500K to £2M mid-sized, £2M to £10M large, £10M+ global. Supports Noel's mid-market, large and global lower bound almost exactly (in GBP). Caveat: an SAP partner, and its own table uses different user-count bands. Cited on page A.
2. LeverX, "SAP S/4HANA migration cost in the U.S." (published 14 Sep 2026, updated 5 Oct 2026): https://leverx.com/en-us/blog/sap-s4hana-migration-cost-us. Large-enterprise greenfield $4M to $10M+; large global transformation $10M to $25M+; small/mid greenfield $150K to $600K+. Broadly supports Noel's bands. Cited on page A.
3. Panorama Consulting 2026 ERP Report press release (4 Mar 2026): more than a quarter of organisations exceeded their ERP budgets; additional technology needs the leading cause. No SAP or size-specific cost figure. Cited on page B as overrun context only.
4. Checked but not cited: ERP Research (erpresearch.com) gives generic ERP implementation-only ranges by employees (upper mid-market $500K to $2M, enterprise $1.5M to $5M+); site has partner lead-generation ties and unattributed figures. Older Panorama reports (2013-2015) give generic ERP averages ($2.8M to $6.5M), not SAP by size.

Verdict: no credible source contradicts Noel's ranges. No neutral analyst benchmark for SAP cost by company size was found; the best public support comes from partner pricing guides.
