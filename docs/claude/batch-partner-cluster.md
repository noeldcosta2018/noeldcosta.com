## Noel's answers (14 October 2026)
Weighs client references most. Walks away over weak resource quality and a partner that cannot explain the architecture and plan. Recommends time and materials with effort quantified. SOWs miss detailed assumptions and exclusions. Independent consultant during contracting, for the design and for assessing the partner's documents. Has changed a partner mid-programme because quality resources were not available. Shortlist at least three; selection about 12 weeks. References are important; ask about experience on similar projects. Vendor neutral.
