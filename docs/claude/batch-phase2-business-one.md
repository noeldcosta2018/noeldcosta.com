## Sources (checked 13 October 2026)

1. CostBench, SAP Business One pricing: https://www.costbench.com/software/erp/sap-business-one/ ("Last verified Aug 31, 2026"; page also says "as of October 2026"). Perpetual $3,213 Professional, $1,666 Limited, $1,357 Starter; cloud multi-tenant $188 / $112 / $110, single-tenant $219 / $158 / $149 per user per month; maintenance 18 to 20%; minimum 5 users for one year on cloud. Aggregator, not SAP; its own dates are inconsistent.
2. Cloud Factory (Inecom Pty Ltd, Melbourne; listed as an SAP Business One partner), public price page: https://www.cloudfactory.co/solutions/erp/sap-business-one/pricing (no price date; footer 2026; currency not stated, likely AUD). Perpetual one-off excluding support: Professional $3,786, Financial or CRM user $1,963, Starter $1,599. Subscription: Professional $213, Financial or CRM $187, Starter $138 a month.
3. ERP Research, SAP Business One pricing: https://erpresearch.com/pricing/sap-business-one (no date; 2026 in title). Cloud $95 to $250 per user per month; perpetual $3,500 to $5,500 per named user; maintenance about 18 to 20%; implementation $15K to $150K, 3 to 6 months. Mentioned, not linked (external link budget).
4. SAP Support, Business One maintenance: https://support.sap.com/en/offerings-programs/support-small-medium-enterprises/business-one/maintenance.html (no page date). Release 10.0 mainstream maintenance to 31 December 2028; "Version 11 to follow after Version 10".
5. ASUG, "B1 SAP Business One pricing and licensing changes you need to know" (14 June 2018): https://asug.com/insights/b1-sap-business-one-pricing-and-licensing-changes-you-need-to-know. User types Professional, Limited, Mobile, Starter Package; indirect access price list items "per User" and "per Instance" from 16 April 2018. Used for structure only, no price.
6. New Straits Times / Bernama (6 June 2025): https://www.nst.com.my/amp/news/nation/2025/06/1226501/lhdn-e-invoice-implementation-revenue-below-rm5mil-deferred-jan-1-2026. LHDN phases: RM5M to RM25M from 1 July 2025; RM1M to RM5M from 1 January 2026; up to RM1M from 1 July 2026; under RM500,000 exempt.
7. Appcentric Solutions (Philippine SAP partner blog, 11 July 2026): https://www.appcentric.ph/blog/sap-cas-accreditation-bir-eis-philippines. CAS registration ends in an Acknowledgement Certificate (RMC 5-2021, RMO 9-2021); RR 11-2025 covers CAS users with invoicing modules for EIS; mentions a December 2026 e-invoicing deadline (not used in the article).
8. sap.com Business One product page linked as before; it returned HTTP 403 to automated fetch, so nothing on it was verified this batch.
