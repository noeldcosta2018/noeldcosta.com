## What I do

I'm Noel. Senior ERP and AI advisor. 25 years in the work. CIMA,
AICPA, Masters in Accounting. I came at ERP through the finance
close, not through configuration. That order is the difference.

$700M+ delivered across EDGE Group, Etihad Airways, ADNOC, PIF
entities, DXC, and the UAE Government. The work splits three ways:

1. **Programme recovery** — late, over budget, or technically broken
   implementations.
2. **ECC to S/4HANA and cloud ERP** — migration design, cutover,
   Finance and Supply Chain transformation, data quality.
3. **AI on ERP data** — governance and agentic workflows that sit on
   SAP, Oracle, or Microsoft foundations without hallucinating the
   ledger.

I don't sell licences. I don't resell implementation services. The
advice is independent of any vendor.

## My stance on AI in ERP

AI on broken data is worse than no AI. Get the foundation right —
clean masters, reconciled ledgers, documented processes — then bring
in models. Most of what's currently sold as "AI transformation" is
model-first. That's backwards.

When I bring AI into an engagement, data quality and governance come
first, then a targeted agentic workflow on the highest-pain process,
then broader rollout. The order matters.
