## What I help with

### ERP selection

I start with the problems you need to solve, then your industry and size. That rules out half the market. I narrow the field to two or three realistic options and ask each vendor to demonstrate your own workflows, not a generic demo.

### Implementation support and recovery

I plan programmes that hold their budget and dates, and I step in when one has slipped. On SAP I follow SAP Activate. On Oracle I use the Oracle Unified Method. I prefer phased rollouts to a single cutover, and I keep the old system running in parallel while the new one proves itself.

### Licences and contracts

Vendors price in ways that are easy to get wrong. SAP licenses by user type, Oracle often by processor core, and Microsoft changes its Dynamics pricing every few years. I work out what you actually need before you negotiate, then review the contract for usage, maintenance costs, timeline penalties and your rights to your own data.

### AI strategy

I find the few AI use cases worth funding, build a roadmap that starts small, and set the governance that keeps company data safe.

## Questions people ask

### Can we change systems without disrupting the business?

Yes, with planning. I start with the people who do the work every day, agree what must keep running, and phase the rollout. Training starts well before the switch. Something always goes a little sideways. Good planning keeps it small.

### Which ERP should we choose?

It depends on your problems, industry, size and budget, including implementation cost. Most companies end up with two or three realistic options. Seeing your own processes in each one usually makes the choice clear.

### Do you help negotiate licences with the vendor?

Yes. It is often where I save clients the most. Vendors offer better terms when they see that you understand their pricing models.
